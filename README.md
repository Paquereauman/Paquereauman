### 🥖 [Français A1 · Je parle baguette (法语学习)](https://paquereauman.github.io/apprendre-francais-a1/)

[![Live App](https://img.shields.io/badge/Web%20App-Live%20Demo-2ea44f?style=flat-square&logo=safari&logoColor=white)](https://paquereauman.github.io/apprendre-francais-a1/)
[![Source Code](https://img.shields.io/badge/Source%20Code-GitHub-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Paquereauman/apprendre-francais-a1)
![CEFR A1](https://img.shields.io/badge/Level-CEFR%20A1-blue?style=flat-square)
![Language](https://img.shields.io/badge/Bilingual-FR%20%7C%20%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87-orange?style=flat-square)

An **interactive web application** tailored for Chinese speakers to learn French at the CEFR A1 level, combining structured progression with engaging gamification:

- 📚 **17 progressive chapters**: ~760 illustrated vocabulary words with emojis and 25 essential communicative patterns.
- 🗣️ **Neural audio pronunciation**: high-fidelity Microsoft cognitive voices paired with contextual thematic posters.
- 🎯 **Server-graded assessments**: anti-cheat safeguards with server-side validation and monthly community leaderboards.
- 🔄 **Spaced repetition engine** and interactive vocabulary drills for long-term retention.
- 🧑‍🎨 **Customizable avatars**: daily gifts unlocking collectible accessories, outfits, and hairstyles.
- 📱 **Zero-install & responsive**: instant access across mobile phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

### ⚡ [IP Unlocker](https://github.com/Paquereauman/cu-bootstrap)

[![Windows](https://img.shields.io/badge/Windows-10%20%2F%2011-0078D4?style=flat-square&logo=windows&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Paquereauman/cu-bootstrap/releases/latest/download/IP-Unlocker-Windows.exe)
[![macOS Apple Silicon](https://img.shields.io/badge/macOS-Apple%20Silicon%20(M1--M4)-000000?style=flat-square&logo=apple&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Paquereauman/cu-bootstrap/releases/latest/download/IP-Unlocker-Mac-AppleSilicon.zip)
[![macOS Intel](https://img.shields.io/badge/macOS-Intel-555555?style=flat-square&logo=apple&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Paquereauman/cu-bootstrap/releases/latest/download/IP-Unlocker-Mac-Intel.zip)
![Engine](https://img.shields.io/badge/Engine-Sing--box%20TUN%20L3-14524c?style=flat-square)

A high-performance **cross-platform desktop utility for Windows and macOS** built to unblock AI platforms (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) and bypass geographic network restrictions seamlessly:

- 🛡️ **Silent Layer 3 TUN Engine** (Sing-box): intercepts network packets at the IP layer without touching fragile Windows proxy registry keys (`ProxyEnable = 0`), preventing crashes and browser disconnects.
- 🔀 **Smart Dual-Routing**: automatically routes AI traffic through Cloudflare WARP relay while general web traffic flows through your regular VPN upstream.
- 🔌 **Universal Auto-Detection**: automatically probes and attaches to active local cores (`v2rayN`, `Clash Verge Rev`, `FlClash`, `Shadowsocks`) with native UDP associate handling.
- 🔒 **Zero WebRTC Leak**: built-in protection against real IP disclosure during AI chat sessions.
- 📦 **Native Installers**: one-click install with automatic update checks on both Windows and macOS.

## 👋 About Me

I trained in **techno-anthropology** in **Copenhagen** — studying how people and technology shape each other — and I now work as a **teacher**. I originally learned Python for **web scraping** to collect data for field research.

Today I build pragmatic tools **with Claude**. My approach is simple: observe a real everyday friction around me, understand what users truly need, and turn it into a lightweight, reliable tool that helps someone.

### 🧭 Core Principles
- 🔎 **Observe first**: my techno-anthropology training helps me understand what people actually need.
- 🦽 **Accessibility & usefulness**: practical solutions for education, accessibility, and daily workflows.
- 🌱 **Learn by doing**: every project teaches me new engineering patterns.
